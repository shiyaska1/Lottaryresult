## Full description (max 4000 chars)

**Lottery Result Print** turns the Kerala State Lotteries result into a single,
dense, bold, print-ready page — instead of the official 4-page PDF — so shop
owners can print or share a result in seconds.

**What it does**
• Pick a lottery from a dropdown — SAMRUDHI, KARUNYA, KARUNYA PLUS,
  STHREE-SAKTHI, DHANALEKSHMI, SUVARNA KERALAM, BHAGYATHARA and the current
  bumper draw — fetched from the Kerala Government's own lottery portal.
• Add your own shop or business name as the page header.
• Get back one compact A4 page with every prize tier laid out in a clean,
  bold, high-contrast gazette style — auto-sized to fit a single sheet,
  never spilling onto a second page.
• Print it directly, download it as a PDF or JPG, or share it straight to
  WhatsApp.
• Optionally password-protect the PDF before sharing.
• Works from an imported PDF too, if you already have the official result
  file.

**Why one page**
The official result PDF is 4 loosely-spaced pages. This app reflows the
exact same prize data — 1st/2nd/3rd prizes, consolation, and every numbered
tier — onto one tightly packed, legible page that's cheaper and faster to
print, and easier to send over WhatsApp.

**Free trial**
The app is free to try for 30 days. After that, activation requires a
one-time key tied to your device — contact us in-app (WhatsApp button
included) to get one.

**A note on accuracy**
This app reformats results fetched from the Kerala Government's own lottery
portal, but it is an independent tool, not an official government
publication. Every generated page reminds ticket holders to verify winning
numbers against the official Kerala Government Gazette before making any
claim.
